# CARS24 Australia — Outside-In Builder Diagnostic

> What would I investigate, prioritise and build if I joined CARS24 Australia tomorrow?

This repository is an independent, outside-in exploration of CARS24 Australia's business and the broader Australian used-car market.

It is not intended to pretend that public information can substitute for internal company data.

Instead, the goal is to demonstrate a Builder's process:

**Research → Evidence → Problems → Hypotheses → Economics → Prioritisation → Experiments → Build**

## The Question

CARS24 has already demonstrated that technology and AI can transform the car buying and selling experience.

The more interesting question is:

**Where should CARS24 Australia deploy its next dollar, person, process improvement or agent?**

Rather than begin with a solution, this project begins with the market.

## Phase 1 — Understand

Research:

- Australian used-car market
- CARS24 Australia's business model
- customer journey
- competitors
- customer reviews
- operating model
- company and hiring signals
- recent strategic developments

## Phase 2 — Diagnose

Create an evidence-backed problem landscape.

Every conclusion is classified as:

### OBSERVED
Directly supported by public evidence.

### INFERRED
A hypothesis supported by multiple signals but requiring validation.

### SYNTHETIC
Modelled using explicit assumptions to understand potential economics.

### UNKNOWN
Requires internal CARS24 data.

## Phase 3 — Prioritise

Potential opportunities are evaluated across:

- customer impact
- revenue impact
- margin impact
- working-capital impact
- frequency
- strategic importance
- confidence
- implementation effort
- time to measurable result

## Phase 4 — Experiment

For the highest-priority problems:

**Problem → Root-cause hypothesis → Baseline → Intervention → KPI → Target → Duration → Stop condition**

## Phase 5 — Build

Only after the problem is sufficiently understood will this repository develop software, automation or AI prototypes.

Potential areas may include:

- inventory intelligence
- pricing
- vehicle trust
- seller conversion
- refurbishment operations
- customer experience
- finance conversion
- logistics
- after-sales
- operational intelligence

The research decides what gets built.

## Data Integrity

No confidential or proprietary CARS24 information is used.

Public sources are referenced wherever possible.

Any simulated operational data will be clearly labelled **SYNTHETIC**.

Assumptions will be documented rather than presented as facts.

## Why This Exists

This is also my application for a Business Builder role at CARS24.

Rather than write about how I approach ambiguous businesses, I wanted to show it.

The final output should answer three questions:

1. **What appears to matter most?**
2. **Why do I believe that?**
3. **What would I do about it?**
