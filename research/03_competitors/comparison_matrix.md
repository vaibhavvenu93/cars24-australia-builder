# CARS24 Australia — Competitive Model Comparison

_Last updated: August 2026_

## Purpose

This document compares the structural alternatives available to an Australian used-car buyer or seller.

The objective is not to decide which company is "best."

It is to understand:

- where CARS24 has structural advantages
- where competitors have structural advantages
- what customers trade off between models
- which advantages are difficult to copy
- which disadvantages CARS24 must compensate for operationally

Models compared:

1. CARS24
2. Carma
3. Carsales
4. Traditional dealer
5. Private transaction

---

# 1. The Five Competing Models

## CARS24

Vertically integrated digital retailer.

CARS24:

- acquires vehicles
- owns inventory
- inspects
- refurbishes
- prices
- sells
- arranges finance
- delivers
- provides warranty
- provides eligible returns.

Primary promise:

**A simpler and more trusted digital transaction.**

---

## Carma

Vertically integrated digital used-car retailer.

Structurally similar to CARS24:

- direct sourcing
- owned inventory
- inspection
- reconditioning
- finance
- delivery
- returns
- wholesale exit channel.

Primary promise:

**High-confidence online vehicle buying and selling with strong quality control.**

---

## Carsales

Primarily a marketplace / transaction infrastructure model.

Carsales connects:

- private buyers
- private sellers
- dealers
- wholesale buyers.

It increasingly provides:

- vehicle valuation
- Instant Offer
- identity verification
- secure payments
- dealer-at-home services
- research and comparison.

Carsales currently displays more than 200,000 new and used vehicles.

Its major advantage is:

# liquidity.

---

## Traditional Dealer

Physical retailer.

Common strengths:

- inspect vehicle physically
- test drive
- trade-in
- finance
- negotiation
- immediate human reassurance
- local after-sales support.

Primary advantage:

**physical trust and human interaction.**

---

## Private Sale

Buyer and seller transact directly.

Potential advantage:

**highest seller price / lowest buyer intermediary margin.**

Potential disadvantages:

- fraud
- negotiation
- inspection uncertainty
- payment risk
- documentation
- no retailer-backed return proposition
- limited after-sales recourse.

# 3. CARS24's Strongest Buyer Advantage

CARS24's most distinctive public proposition is currently its:

# 30-Day Return Guarantee

Eligible CARS24-owned vehicles delivered from 19 February 2026 receive:

- 30 calendar days
- up to 1,000 km

to decide whether the vehicle is right for the buyer.

Days 1–7:

eligible full refund.

Days 8–30:

eligible refund less the listed service/restocking fee in normal circumstances.

This substantially reduces a customer's fear of making the wrong digital purchase.

---

# 4. Carma's Return Proposition

Carma offers a:

**7-day return guarantee**

subject to eligibility conditions.

Its standard allowance is:

**500 kilometres**

during the return period before excess-mileage terms may apply.

Therefore CARS24 currently offers materially more customer experimentation time.

This may be a genuine conversion advantage.

But it also creates additional:

- inventory risk
- reverse logistics
- refurbishment
- capital exposure

if returns occur.

Therefore the 30-day policy is simultaneously:

**a trust feature**

and

**an operational risk product.**

---

# 5. Carsales Has a Very Different Advantage: Choice

Carsales currently presents more than:

**200,000 vehicles**

across new and used listings.

A vertically integrated retailer cannot practically own inventory of comparable breadth.

This gives Carsales a structural discovery advantage.

A customer asking:

> Which used Toyota RAV4 should I buy?

can compare a huge market.

CARS24 instead needs to answer:

> Why should one of my curated cars be easier and safer to purchase?

Therefore CARS24 should probably not attempt to beat marketplaces purely on:

**inventory breadth.**

It must win on:

**transaction quality.**

---

# 6. Carsales Is Moving Down the Transaction Stack

Carsales should not be viewed as merely an advertising website.

Its current product increasingly includes:

- offers
- ID verification
- payments
- buyer/seller messaging
- valuation
- Instant Offer
- dealer fulfilment.

Its payment product holds buyer funds until physical handover and then releases them to the seller.

Carsales says more than:

**A$440M**

has already moved through this secure-payment system.

This is strategically important.

Marketplace competitors can gradually remove friction without taking vehicle inventory risk.

---

# 7. Carsales Instant Offer Directly Competes for Sellers

Carsales now provides:

# Instant Offer

Sellers can:

1. submit vehicle data
2. receive an offer
3. accept within seven days
4. have the vehicle inspected
5. sell to an accredited dealer
6. potentially complete the transaction within approximately 24 hours.

Carsales says the service is powered by live market data and RedBook information.

This directly attacks one of CARS24's seller-side advantages:

**speed + certainty + no private-sale hassle.**

# 9. Seller Competition Is Becoming Intense

Seller acquisition is strategically important because good inventory starts with good supply.

CARS24 competes against:

- Carma direct sourcing
- Carsales Instant Offer
- dealership trade-ins
- dealer purchasing
- private sale
- auction/wholesale channels.

Carsales publicly claims its Instant Offer can complete a transaction in approximately 24 hours and that 83% of Instant Offers exceed a trade-in offer according to its own internal comparison.

Carma positions its seller proposition around:

- live market pricing
- instant payment
- no strangers
- no test drives
- no "lowballing or luring."

The messaging itself tells us what sellers fear.

---

# 10. The Real Seller Trade-Off

A seller effectively chooses between:

## Maximise Price

Private sale.

versus:

## Maximise Convenience / Certainty

CARS24 / Carma / Instant Offer / dealer trade-in.

Therefore CARS24 does not necessarily need to offer the highest theoretical price.

It needs the customer's perceived:

# Convenience Value

to exceed the price difference.

This implies a possible formula:

Seller Utility

=

Final Offer

+

Convenience Value

+

Speed Value

+

Risk Reduction

−

Expectation Gap.

# 11. Traditional Dealers Still Have One Major Structural Advantage

A dealership allows customers to:

- see the car
- sit in it
- inspect it
- test drive it
- speak directly to someone
- negotiate
- drive away.

This reduces the psychological uncertainty inherent in digital purchasing.

Digital retailers compensate with:

- inspection
- condition reports
- photography
- warranties
- returns
- delivery.

Therefore one of CARS24's central operating requirements is:

> Manufacture enough trust digitally that physical dealership interaction becomes unnecessary.

---

# 12. Private Sale Has an Economic Advantage

Private transactions avoid much of the intermediary margin embedded in:

- dealer acquisition
- refurbishment
- facilities
- sales staff
- warranty
- delivery
- marketing.

Therefore:

Seller may receive more.

Buyer may pay less.

But the participants absorb more:

- search effort
- fraud risk
- condition risk
- inspection cost
- negotiation
- payment coordination
- paperwork.

CARS24 essentially monetises:

# Complexity Removal.

# 15. CARS24 Cannot Win by Copying Carsales

Carsales benefits from:

# Asset-light scale.

It does not need to buy every car to monetise buyer intent.

Therefore CARS24 trying to maximise inventory breadth without regard to velocity would create poor capital economics.

A better strategy is potentially:

# Curated Availability

Own the inventory with the highest expected economic return and customer relevance.

This directly strengthens our Vehicle Lifecycle Intelligence thesis.

---

# 16. CARS24 Cannot Win Only by Copying Dealers

Traditional dealers have physical proximity.

CARS24's advantages should instead come from:

- superior information
- reduced negotiation
- better digital experience
- faster workflow
- broader network data
- more consistent trust.

Technology needs to compensate for lower physical interaction.

---

# 17. CARS24 Cannot Win Only by Paying Sellers More

Aggressively increasing seller offers can increase supply.

But it may also destroy:

- acquisition margin
- pricing flexibility
- ability to absorb refurbishment
- inventory economics.

Therefore seller conversion must be optimised against:

# Lifetime Vehicle Economics.

Not isolated acquisition volume.

# 22. Competitive Insight: Trust Alone Is Not Enough

Both CARS24 and Carma now have strong trust propositions.

Traditional dealers provide physical trust.

Carsales is adding ID verification and secure payments.

Therefore:

**trust is becoming table stakes.**

The durable advantage may be:

> using trust data to improve economics.

For example:

inspection → warranty prediction.

or:

condition confidence → conversion.

That moves beyond marketing.

---

# 23. Competitive Insight: Seller Convenience Is Also Converging

CARS24:

fast valuation + managed inspection + payment.

Carma:

fast valuation + direct purchase + instant payment.

Carsales:

Instant Offer + inspection + accredited dealer + payment.

Therefore a simple:

**"sell your car quickly"**

proposition is increasingly commoditised.

Winning may depend on:

- better valuation
- fewer offer surprises
- better seller experience
- better use of acquired vehicles.

# 24. Competitive Insight: Operational Excellence Becomes the Differentiator

As customer propositions converge, internal execution becomes more important.

Two companies can provide similar:

- warranties
- online purchase
- finance
- delivery.

But one may make materially more money because it:

- buys better vehicles
- refurbishes faster
- prices better
- turns inventory faster
- predicts warranty risk better.

This is exactly where our Builder investigation should concentrate.

# 26. Most Important Strategic Conclusion

CARS24's biggest structural weakness is also potentially its biggest opportunity:

# It owns the car.

Carsales avoids inventory risk.

CARS24 embraces it.

That means every vehicle creates:

- capital risk
- depreciation risk
- operating complexity.

But ownership also gives CARS24 control over:

- condition
- refurbishment
- pricing
- presentation
- finance
- delivery
- after-sales
- data.

The winner is therefore not the company that owns the most cars.

It is potentially the company that:

# makes the best decisions about every car it owns.
